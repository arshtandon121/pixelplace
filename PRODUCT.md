# PixelPlace

A public 50×50 pixel canvas where businesses and creators rent space to display a logo and a website link. Listings go live after payment. Plans run from 1 hour to 1 year; month and year can auto-renew.

This is advertising / brand display on a hosted page. It is not an investment, game, NFT, or marketplace for other sellers.

Audience: founders and marketers who want a visible mark on a public grid. Voice: precise, physical, unhurried. No hype about appreciation or ownership of digital land.
